Is this the report Odarri exported?

Odarri certifies every PDF report on the phone that made it. Drop the PDF here to see whether it has changed since it was exported. The check runs in this page; nothing is uploaded.

CSV export

The PDF prints the SHA-256 of the CSV made from the same report. Paste that number, then drop the CSV.

What this shows: whether the file is the one Odarri exported, byte for byte. It does not show who made the report (the signing key belongs to one phone and no authority vouches for it), and it does not show that the drives are true. Odarri is not tax advice.
