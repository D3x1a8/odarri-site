Missed a drive?

Open Odarri › Tracking Health first. It lists anything that can stop a drive being caught, with a button to fix each one. The usual causes:

  1. Location set to While Using. Settings › Odarri › Location › Always, with Precise Location on.
  2. Motion & Fitness off. Without it, drives start late and short stops can split one.
  3. Background App Refresh off, or Low Power Mode on. iOS wakes Odarri less often.
  4. The app was force-quit. Swiping Odarri away stops iOS waking it. Open it once to re-arm detection.

Still missing one? Add it by hand in the log, and email us the date and rough time. Every missed drive we hear about becomes a test case before it's fixed.

Questions

Is it really free with unlimited drives?

Yes. Automatic and manual drives, rules, Apple Watch, widgets, iCloud sync and every PDF report are free, with no limit. That won't change.

Does it drain my battery?

When you're not driving, Odarri doesn't run GPS. iOS wakes it when you move, and it records the route only while you drive.

How much is Odarri Pro?

$9.99 for your first year, then $14.99 a year. Or $29.99 once, for good. No monthly plan. Pro adds CSV of any period, more than one vehicle, client rules and custom purposes.

I bought Pro on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open Odarri Pro in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Odarri Pro. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new iPhone

Keep iCloud on for Odarri and your log arrives on the new iPhone by itself. Settings › Export everything (JSON) keeps a full copy of your own as well.

Coming from MileIQ, Everlance or another app?

Export a CSV there, then Settings › Import drives (CSV) in Odarri. It reads exports from MileIQ, Everlance, Driversnote, Stride, TripLog or a spreadsheet.

Which mileage rates are built in?

The US (IRS), UK (HMRC), Canada (CRA), Australia (ATO) and New Zealand (IRD). Anywhere else, set your own rate per mile or kilometre.

How does someone check my report?

They drop the PDF on odarri.com/verify. It shows whether the file is the one Odarri exported, byte for byte. It doesn't prove the drives happened, and Odarri isn't tax advice.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your log lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
