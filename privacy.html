We don't collect your data. Here is exactly what that means.

Who we are

Odarri is made by Pritesh Singh, an independent developer. Questions about this policy go to support@odarri.com.

What stays on your devices

Your drives (routes, times, distances, places, purposes and reasons), your rules, vehicles and settings are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database. Apple holds that data under your Apple Account; we cannot read it.

Location and motion

Odarri asks for location (Always) and Motion & Fitness so it can tell when you drive and measure the route. Both are processed on your iPhone. You can say no and log drives by hand; the app works fully in manual mode.

What Apple sees

To turn the start and end of a drive into an address and to measure road distance, Odarri uses Apple Maps. Those requests send coordinates to Apple, not to us, under Apple's privacy policy. Purchases go through the App Store; we receive no name, email or payment details. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Reports you share

PDF and CSV reports are made on your iPhone and go only where you send them. The check page at odarri.com/verify runs in your browser; the file you drop there is never uploaded.

Deleting your data

In Odarri, Settings › Delete everything removes every drive, place, rule and setting from this iPhone and your iCloud. Settings › Export everything (JSON) gives you a full copy first. Deleting the app removes local data; iCloud data can also be removed in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

This website

odarri.com sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare; both keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) to deliver and protect the site.

Children, changes

Odarri is not directed at children. If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date, and a change that collects anything would be announced in the app first.
